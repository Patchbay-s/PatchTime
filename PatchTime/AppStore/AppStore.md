## Description

PatchTime helps you track overtime, time off in lieu, work meals, and leave by
employer, then prepare personal summaries.

Features:

- Record overtime in 15-minute increments, including shifts that cross midnight
- Track work meals, employers, contracts, and typical work weeks
- Set hourly rates and overtime pay rules
- Track paid leave, time off in lieu, and public holidays in France and Monaco
- Review a customizable calendar and statistics
- Prepare indicative annual and tax summaries
- Export monthly PDF and CSV reports; back up and restore data
- Use home-screen, Lock Screen, and employer-specific widgets
- Sync through the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple account

Amounts and summaries are estimates for information only and are not tax advice.
