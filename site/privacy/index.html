PATCHTIME

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Data stored by the app

Overtime, meals, leave, employer and contract details, rate profiles, and preferences are stored in the app's database. PatchTime uses CloudKit to sync that database through the private iCloud account associated with your Apple account. The project maintainers do not have access to that private database.

Widgets read a local summary of app data. Reminders and threshold alerts are scheduled on your device.

Network requests

PatchTime requests French public-holiday data from calendrier.api.gouv.fr. The request does not include your personal records. PatchTime does not include advertising, analytics, or payment SDKs.

Your choices

You can view, change, and export app entries inside PatchTime. Delete entries in the app and manage CloudKit data in your Apple account settings. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, CNIL.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the PatchTime support page. Do not post private records in a public issue.
